Privacy

Short version. No fine print tricks.

Shredball is a paid iOS game. No ads. No analytics we run. No accounts of our own.

Local scores stay on your device. If you use Game Center, Breakout scores and your Game Center ID go to Apple so the leaderboard works. We don’t sell your data. We don’t track you across apps.
